# Legal and tax checklist

Nothing here is legal or tax advice. It is the list of things to verify with official sources and, before the first sale, a one-time consult with a CPA. Record each answer with its source and date.

## Before the first sale

- [ ] **Business structure:** sole proprietorship vs LLC, and when an LLC is worth its state fees and taxes. (CPA)
- [ ] **State and local registration:** whether the owner's state, county and city require a business license for an online-only business, and at what revenue threshold.
- [ ] **Sales tax on digital products:** confirm whether the owner's state taxes SaaS and digital products, and confirm the merchant of record covers collection and remittance everywhere it sells.
- [ ] **Merchant of record:** choose a provider (for example Paddle or Lemon Squeezy). Compare fees, payout timing, and prohibited-business lists.
- [ ] **Separate money:** a separate bank account (and card) for business income and expenses.
- [ ] **Bookkeeping:** a simple system that tracks income and costs per product, so each product can be valued and sold on its own.

## Once there is income

- [ ] **Federal income tax:** business profit reported on Schedule C (sole proprietor) or per the chosen structure.
- [ ] **Self-employment tax:** applies to net self-employment profit on top of income tax.
- [ ] **Quarterly estimated payments:** when they become required, and the safe-harbor rules. Alternatively, increase withholding at the day job.
- [ ] **State tax on business income:** confirm what the owner's state taxes.

## Per product

- [ ] Terms of service and privacy policy suited to what the product actually does.
- [ ] Privacy obligations for the data it collects (consider US state privacy laws, and GDPR if selling to the EU).
- [ ] Marketplace and payment-processor policy check against the product's category.
- [ ] Disclosure that customer support is AI-assisted.
- [ ] Trademark search on the product name before buying a domain.
