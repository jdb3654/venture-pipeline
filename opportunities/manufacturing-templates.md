## The idea in two sentences

Sell well-built Excel and Google Sheets templates for small manufacturers (OEE tracker, downtime log, preventive maintenance schedule, shift report) on Gumroad, Etsy and similar stores. It's the cheapest possible test of whether this buyer will pay, and doubles as a lead source for the downtime tracker.

## Signal

- Comparable templates are on sale; for example, an OEE calculator and tracker for Excel and Google Sheets is listed at €12 on Gumroad, with no sales count shown (checked 2026-10-10). [Gumroad listing](https://devls.gumroad.com/l/oee_calc)
- Counter-evidence: vendors give similar templates away free as lead magnets (for example TeepTrak's free workbook and Learn Lean Sigma's calculator). [TeepTrak](https://teeptrak.com/en/free-downtime-tracking-software/), [Learn Lean Sigma](https://www.learnleansigma.com/template/oee-calculator/)

## Customers and distribution

- **Buyer:** supervisors, CI engineers and small-shop owners.
- **Distribution:** Etsy and Gumroad search, plus search engines. Marketplaces handle checkout.
- **Competition:** many free and cheap templates; differentiation must come from quality and specificity (for example, a template per process type).

## Economics (estimates, not forecasts)

- Price: $10–30 per template, or a bundle.
- Revenue likely small. The point is the first paying customer and a list of interested manufacturers.

## Owner tasks that remain

- Store account setup (owner approval).
- A quick review of formulas for correctness, since the owner knows OEE in practice.

## Validation plan

Publish on Etsy: a 3-template bundle plus each template on its own (4 listings). Run 30 days from listing. Pass: 5+ sales. Cost: $0.80 in listing fees plus Etsy's one-time $29 shop setup fee (seen on Etsy's onboarding screen, 2026-10-10).

Why Etsy over Gumroad (checked 2026-10-10): Etsy buyers search the marketplace, which is the distribution we need; Etsy collects and remits US sales tax as a marketplace facilitator; fees are a $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee and 3% + $0.25 payment processing in the US ([Checkout Page, Jul 2026](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees), a competitor's summary). Gumroad is merchant of record but charges 10% + $0.50 on direct sales and 30% on sales through its own Discover marketplace ([Checkout Page](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees)). Etsy's creativity standards require disclosing AI use in the description ([Etsy](https://etsy.com/legal/creativity)).

Product files, listing copy and verification: `products/manufacturing-templates/`.

## Case against (written before listing)

- Free OEE and downtime templates are easy to find, including from vendors using them as lead magnets, so buyers may never need to pay.
- Etsy buyers skew toward personal and small-business templates; manufacturing supervisors may not shop there at all.
- At $9–24 per sale, even a "pass" (5 sales) is only about $100 of revenue. The real value is learning whether this buyer pays and starting an owned email list. If neither happens, stop quickly rather than adding more templates.

## Kill criteria

Zero sales in 30 days. Then use the templates as free lead magnets for the downtime tracker instead.

## Exit

Not sellable on its own; keep or shut down.
