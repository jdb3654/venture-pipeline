## The idea in two sentences

A guided tool that produces the documents an EU-bound small seller must supply under the General Product Safety Regulation (product risk analysis, safety information, warnings, label text) and keeps them per product. Etsy and Shopify sellers outside the EU who make handmade goods pay a small fee per year.

## Signal

Lenses: forced deadline, picks and shovels for small sellers.

- Sellers must provide their own product descriptions, technical documentation including a risk analysis, test reports, labels and packaging designs, and keep them updated; an EU responsible-person service costs **€150–500/year** [vendor, unsourced range: [Euverify](https://euverify.com/resource/authorised-representative-costs-guide/), 2026-02-20, updated 2026-04-18; Euverify sells this service].
- GPSR has applied since 13 December 2024 [assume: widely reported date, not re-checked this session].

## Customers and distribution

- **Buyer:** US/UK handmade sellers on Etsy and Shopify selling into the EU [assume].
- **Today they use:** EU responsible-person services that also offer document help [vendor: Euverify], free templates (a free GPSR risk-assessment template from 24hour-ar; seller guides from eRank and EaseCert) [fact: search results 2026-10-11, pages not read], or they stop selling to the EU (24hour-ar article title "should you stop selling?").
- **Route without ads:** Shopify App Store has built-in distribution; Etsy has no app store and its API needs app approval (**approval-gated**) [assume].
- **Competitors:** **GPSR Kit** on Shopify, free up to 10 products, **$14.99/month** (500 products) or **$29/month** (unlimited), launched 2026-07-08, **0 reviews** [fact on listing: [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/gpsr-kit)]; responsible-person services such as Euverify and EaseCert [vendor].

## Strongest reason it fails

The part sellers clearly pay for is the EU responsible person, which must be an EU-established operator; we can't provide it. What's left (documents and labels) is available free from responsible-person services and template sites, and many small sellers either complied in the 2024–25 wave or stopped shipping to the EU [assume]. Writing product-safety risk analyses for customers also edges toward regulatory advice, which `AGENTS.md` routes to the owner.
