# Support, refund and discount policy: manufacturing templates

Applies to the TaktTools Etsy listings for the OEE Tracker, Downtime Log & Pareto, PM Schedule and the bundle.
Draft for the owner's approval (#23). Agents work in draft-and-approve mode: they draft replies, the owner sends them.

## What we support

- **Software:** only the apps with a full pass recorded in `tests/xsuite/README.md`. Until a run passes, the listing
  must not claim that app. Target list: Microsoft Excel (desktop and web), Google Sheets, OpenOffice/LibreOffice.
- **Included help:** downloading the files; how to use the templates as described in the guide; explaining a Check or
  status message; fixing a genuine defect in the template.
- **Not included:** custom changes, extra columns or machines beyond the stated limits, macros, connecting to other
  systems, set-up done for the buyer, or help with apps not on the supported list.

## Replies

- **Target:** answer within 2 business days through Etsy messages. No phone or video calls.
- **Routine (agent drafts, owner sends):** download help, how-to questions answered from the guide, "which app does
  it work in", feature requests (thank them and log the request in the repo).
- **Escalate to the owner:** refund or discount requests, complaints, anything legal or security-related, payment
  disputes, and anything the agent is unsure about.

## Defects

If a buyer reports a calculation or formula problem: reproduce it with the cross-app suite, add a scenario that fails,
fix `build.py`, rebuild the files and guides, re-upload them to every listing that carries them, and message the buyer
that a corrected version is in their Etsy purchases. Etsy lets buyers re-download updated files.

## Refunds and discounts

- **Digital downloads are generally non-refundable,** because the file can't be returned. We still refund in full
  when we can't fix a genuine defect, or when the file doesn't work in an app we list as supported.
- **Duplicate purchases** (bought a single template and the bundle by mistake): refund the overlapping single template.
- **No other refunds or discounts** without the owner's decision. No ad-hoc discount codes from agents.
- Set the Etsy shop policy to match before publishing (Etsy's digital-item return settings), and confirm wording
  against Etsy's current policy page at that time.
